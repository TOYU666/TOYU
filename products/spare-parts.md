# Spare Parts

## 中文说明

备件资料页用于整理钻机、空压机、泥浆泵、动力系统和钻具相关配件。

## 英文文案草稿

Spare parts support should be based on confirmed machine model, part number, photos, dimensions, and working condition. For accurate matching, customers are encouraged to provide nameplates, old part photos, and machine information.

## 常见备件

- Engine parts
- Hydraulic parts
- Rotary head parts
- Mud pump parts
- Air compressor parts
- Seals and wear parts
