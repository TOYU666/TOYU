# Drill Pipes

## 中文说明

钻杆资料页用于整理不同规格钻杆、螺纹、材质、长度和适配设备信息。

## 英文文案草稿

Drill pipes are selected based on rig capacity, drilling depth, torque, thread type, pipe diameter, wall thickness, and working conditions. Accurate thread and size confirmation is necessary before quotation or shipment.
